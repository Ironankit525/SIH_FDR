# Risk engine evaluation

Generated 2026-10-25 with risk engine 1.0.0 and the built-in weight policy. Corpus: 120 synthetic cases (60 needing intervention, 60 genuine), seed 7.

> **Read this first.** Every institution, bank record and document here is fictional and generated by `backend/evaluation/corpus.py`. These numbers measure the engine against the problems it was designed for; they are not real-world fraud detection accuracy. Real forgeries will include techniques not modelled here. The score orders review work and never decides a case.

## Headline

Medium is a score of 20 or more; high is 50 or more.

| Stage | AUC | Problem cases ≥ medium | Problem cases ≥ high | Genuine cases ≥ medium | Genuine cases ≥ high | Problem cases in top 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| At intake (documents and declarations only) | 0.989 | 90% | 67% | 0% | 0% | 100% |
| After the simulated bank check | 1.0 | 100% | 80% | 2% | 0% | 100% |

AUC is the chance that a randomly chosen problem case scores above a randomly chosen genuine one (0.5 is guessing, 1.0 is perfect separation).

## What this evaluation changed

The first run (same engine version, earlier corpus with problem cases spread evenly over all institutions) flagged most genuine cases. Four engine flaws were fixed as general rules, then the same corpus was re-run before the corpus itself was changed:

1. Bank names are compared with the normalisation duplicate detection already used ("SBI" = "State Bank of India").
2. Institution history is capped below the medium threshold, so it adds weight to direct evidence but never raises a clean case alone.
3. Reuse and duplicate signals point only at the later submission; the original holder is not the suspect.
4. References compared against OCR readings fold look-alike characters (I/1, O/0, S/5, B/8), and a mismatch counts in proportion to the parser's confidence.

| Earlier corpus | AUC at intake | Genuine ≥ medium at intake | AUC after bank check | Genuine ≥ medium after bank check | Problem ≥ medium after bank check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before the fixes | 0.868 | 23% | 0.877 | 62% | 100% |
| After the fixes | 0.943 | 0% | 1.0 | 5% | 100% |

The corpus was then changed so problems cluster in about 30% of institutions, as they would in practice; the tables above use that corpus.

### Receipt arithmetic check

A forger who raises the principal or extends the term usually leaves the printed maturity value unchanged. The check accepts any common bank convention (simple interest; annual, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly compounding) within 1%, or, with no printed rate, an implied rate of 1–15% a year.

| At intake | Clean rebuilt receipts ≥ medium | All problem cases ≥ medium | AUC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the check (seed 7) | 0% | 80% | 0.946 |
| With the check (seed 7) | 100% | 90% | 0.989 |
| With the check (seed 21) | 83% | 88% | 0.964 |

The check can only fire when a receipt prints its maturity value; one of the corpus's three layouts does not, so expect roughly two-thirds coverage of clean rebuilds rather than the seed-7 figure. Its first run also exposed a corpus bug: genuine coverage-gap receipts printed a maturity value for the wrong term. The generator was fixed; the engine was not changed to accommodate it.

## Limits of this evaluation

- The same team wrote the detectors and the corpus, so the corpus contains the problems the detectors were built for. Treat high scores here as a regression baseline, not an accuracy claim.
- Documents use three receipt layouts with clean typography. Real bank receipts, stamps, handwriting, skewed photographs and regional languages are not represented.
- Fabricated receipts that are internally consistent leave no trace in the document, and clean rebuilds whose receipts omit the maturity value pass the arithmetic check; only bank confirmation exposes them. Without a live bank channel those cases depend on officers obtaining confirmation.
- The bank check is simulated against the corpus's own records.

## Method

Each case goes through the real API: the institution creates the deposit, uploads the receipt and submits; the score is read (intake). A verification officer then runs the simulated bank check against what the bank actually holds, every case is rescored once all exist (as the daily sweep would), and the score is read again. A submission refused as a duplicate counts as detected with score 100.

Reproduce from `backend/`: `../.venv/bin/python -m evaluation`. The regression gate (`tests/test_risk_evaluation.py`) runs a smaller corpus and fails if detection falls below `evaluation/baseline.json`.
